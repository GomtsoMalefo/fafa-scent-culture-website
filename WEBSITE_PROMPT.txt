CLEAN WEBSITE BUILD PROMPT — FAFA SCENT CULTURE

Build a polished, responsive, mobile-first e-commerce-style website for FAFA Scent Culture, a South African fragrance business with the brand direction “Luxury Meets Kasi Culture.” Use the tagline “Smell Expensive. Move Different.” The design should blend premium fragrance-boutique luxury with authentic modern Johannesburg/kasi energy: near-black backgrounds, metallic gold accents, bold expressive typography, subtle street-art textures, warm cinematic lighting, and editorial product photography. Keep the layout elegant and readable rather than cluttered or stereotypical.

TECH STACK
- Plain HTML5, CSS3 and vanilla JavaScript.
- No build step, framework, API key, or paid dependency.
- Provide index.html, style.css, script.js, README.md and an images folder.
- Make the site responsive, accessible, keyboard-friendly, and fast.

SECTIONS AND FUNCTIONALITY
1. Announcement bar and navigation with mobile menu.
2. Hero with “Smell expensive. Move different.”, a strong call to action, and a premium fragrance visual.
3. Fragrance collection with filter buttons and product cards. Show sizes as labelled; sizes vary.
4. Brand story: “Kasi roots. Luxury scents. Unforgettable presence.”
5. Delivery section with PEP PAXI, Bolt Parcel and Uber Parcel. State clearly that delivery costs extra and must be quoted/confirmed.
6. Contact section and footer.
7. Product order buttons open a pre-filled WhatsApp message to +27 74 488 4815. Display both contact numbers: 074 488 4815 and 070 738 0103.

PRODUCTS, PRICES AND SIZES
- Use all product names and prices exactly as listed in the README.md Product catalogue section.
- The README catalogue is the source of truth. Display supplied prices in South African rand (ZAR); mark products without a supplied price as “Ask for price.”
- Product sizes vary. Refer to each product label and do not claim a size that is not shown on that product.
- Product photos show the supplied products and their original packaging.
Do not invent discounts, stock status, reviews, delivery prices, payment methods or product claims. Use tasteful CSS placeholder artwork only for products without supplied photos.

BRAND VOICE
Confident, warm, aspirational, locally grounded, premium, and direct. Avoid fake luxury claims. Use concise copy. Keep “Luxury Meets Kasi Culture” visible.

QUALITY CHECKS
- Verify all links and selectors work.
- Check mobile and desktop layouts.
- Ensure product filters work and WhatsApp order messages include the correct product and price.
- Ensure all images have useful alt text and the website remains usable if a photo fails to load.
- Include simple instructions for opening in VS Code and publishing with GitHub Pages.
